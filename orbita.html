Órbita

Your emotional universe, one day at a time.

An Android application that turns daily emotional records into a planetary interface. Each entry brings together an emotion, its intensity, an energy level and personal notes, making a journal something you can explore visually.

The idea

A journal you can navigate

Feelings do not fit into a single number. Órbita separates the emotion itself from how intense it feels and how much energy accompanies it. A calm day can still be energetic; a difficult feeling can be mild or overwhelming. Recording these dimensions separately keeps the entry more expressive.

The planetary metaphor connects these records to a monthly view. Instead of reading a long list, users can browse their emotional universe and select an entry to revisit its date and details.

Application walkthrough

See Órbita in action

From a feeling to a saved entry

The recording screen combines emotion selection with intensity and energy controls. Notes add the context that a visual marker cannot express on its own.

The calendar brings those entries together. Selecting a planet opens the corresponding record so users can revisit what they saved.

This project focuses on personal reflection and expressive interface design. Its visualizations represent user-entered data; they do not infer emotions or produce clinical assessments.

Core experience

Four parts of the interaction

01 / RECORD

Name the emotion

Choose from a broader emotional vocabulary, including feelings such as calm, peace, disappointment and desolation. The aim is to give users more room to describe their experience than a simple positive-or-negative choice.

02 / ADJUST

Describe intensity and energy

Use separate controls for emotional intensity and energy. These values become visual properties, allowing an entry to communicate more than its category alone.

03 / EXPLORE

Browse the monthly universe

Daily markers organize records by date. The current day receives visual emphasis, while future days appear in gray to distinguish them from recorded entries.

04 / REVISIT

Tap a planet for its details

Open an entry from the calendar to review its date, emotion, intensity, energy and notes. The overview and detail screen work together: one supports browsing, the other preserves context.

Visual logic

How emotional data becomes a planet

Órbita uses direct mappings from recorded values to visual properties. This keeps the interface explainable: users control the information that determines how their entries appear.

EmotionAssociated colorDistinguishes emotional categories in the overview.

Colors summarize categories. Labels and record details provide the precise information behind each planet.

Engineering decisions

Technologies and why they fit

Kotlin / Android

Kotlin provides the foundation for the application, including screen behavior, input handling and the data associated with emotional records. Developing for Android makes the journal available in the everyday context of a mobile device.

Jetpack Compose

Compose supports a declarative interface: controls and visual elements reflect the current application state. This fits a screen where changing a slider should update the related value and its visual representation.

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication supports account access. Authentication is handled as a distinct part of the experience, separate from selecting emotions and exploring records.

Google Sign-In

Google account sign-in offers an account access path without requiring a separate password form in the application. The login flow leads into the emotional recording experience.

My contribution

Connecting software and visual expression

I developed Órbita as a project that combines Android programming with an expressive approach to personal data. My work includes the planetary interface concept, emotional recording controls, calendar navigation, record details and Google account authentication integration.

The central design challenge is balancing a creative metaphor with clear interaction. A planet should invite exploration, while its associated record should remain easy to understand. Separating emotion, intensity and energy helps preserve that clarity.

Further development

Where the project can grow

Future improvements could include accessible color alternatives, configurable reminders, personal data export and summaries across longer periods. These are development directions rather than claims about the current implementation.
