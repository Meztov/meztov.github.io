About me

I am a Software Engineer with a Master's degree in Electronic Engineering, focused on Signal Processing. I develop interactive applications and computer vision projects, combining software engineering, image processing, and creative problem-solving.

My interests include image analysis, gesture-based interaction, and visual interfaces. I enjoy turning technical ideas into applications that people can explore and use.

I also bring experience teaching programming, robotics, and digital design. Working with learners of different ages has helped me explain complex concepts clearly and design activities with users in mind.

Master's research — Foreground–background segmentation

During my master's research, I worked with Markov Random Fields for foreground–background segmentation. This approach combines image information with spatial relationships between neighboring pixels to support consistent segmentation.

This work strengthened my foundation in probabilistic modeling, signal processing, and computer vision, connecting mathematical models with practical image analysis.

02 / Gesture-controlled experiences

Chimuelo

An interactive collection of educational activities controlled through hand gestures. Storytelling, drawing, maze navigation, and Simon Says connect computer vision with creative play.

All activities use MediaPipe-based vision input. The Story Oracle also uses AI-powered speech transcription to turn children's own narrated stories into Word documents.

Technologies: Python, Processing, MediaPipe, and UDP communication.

03 / Android development

Órbita

An Android emotional journal that transforms daily records into an interactive planetary interface.

Users can record emotions, intensity, energy, and personal notes, then explore their entries through a monthly calendar. Selecting a planet opens the corresponding record and its details.

Technologies: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Firebase Authentication, and Google Sign-In.
