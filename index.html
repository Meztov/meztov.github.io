About me

I am a Software Engineer with a Master's degree in Electronic Engineering, focused on Signal Processing. I develop interactive applications and computer vision projects, combining software engineering, image processing, and creative problem-solving.

During my master's research, I worked with Markov Random Fields for foreground–background segmentation. This approach uses relationships between neighboring pixels to support image segmentation, combining image information with spatial context. This work strengthened my foundation in probabilistic modeling, image analysis, and computer vision.

My projects include a virtual fitting room for garment visualization, gesture-controlled educational activities, and an Android application for recording and exploring emotions. I enjoy connecting technical ideas with visual interfaces that people can understand and interact with.

I also bring experience teaching programming, robotics, and digital design. Developing activities for different ages has helped me explain complex concepts clearly and design interactive experiences with users in mind.
